For Amy, on her birthday

Dear Amy,

Another year has quietly turned over, and it still feels like there should be more ceremony to that than a candle and a wish. So here's a little more ceremony.

You have a way of making ordinary days feel less ordinary — the kind of person people call first with good news, and the kind who actually picks up when it's bad. That's rarer than it sounds, and it doesn't go unnoticed.

Whatever this year brings, we hope it hands you back a little of what you give everyone else without even trying. Today, though, isn't about anyone else. Let it be a little indulgent.

Happy birthday, Amy. We're genuinely glad you were born.

With love,

The people lucky enough to know you
